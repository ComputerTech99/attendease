How it works

  1. Percentage = attended ÷ held × 100.
  2. If you are at or above the target, it counts how many classes you can skip in a row and still stay at or above it.
  3. If you are below, it counts how many classes in a row you must attend to get back to the target.
  4. Use “What if I miss…” to preview your percentage before you skip.
  5. After each class, tap “+ Attended today” or “+ Missed today” to keep counts up to date.
  6. “Copy summary” copies all subjects as text, ready to send to your mentor or parents.
